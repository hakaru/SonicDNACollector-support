### Project Studios Are the New Standard

Over **70% of professional-quality music** is now produced in project studio environments. The paradigm has shifted from large commercial studios to home and private setups, putting analog gear directly in the hands of more creators than ever.

The Eurorack modular format continues its explosive growth trajectory. The ability to combine individual modules into limitless sound design configurations appeals strongly to experimentally-minded creators.

## Why Market Growth Drives Sound Analysis Demand

### Understanding Unit-to-Unit Variation

A defining characteristic of analog gear is that **no two units sound exactly alike**. Capacitor aging, subtle resistor tolerance differences, and transformer variation combine to give each piece of equipment its own sonic fingerprint.

As the market expands and more creators gain access to analog gear, the desire to know exactly what acoustic characteristics their specific unit possesses is growing.

### Objective Evaluation for the Used Market

The secondhand analog gear market is booming. However, objective methods for evaluating used equipment have been limited. Subjective descriptions like "fat-sounding" or "bright" are insufficient for making informed purchase decisions.

If you can quantify acoustic characteristics, both sellers and buyers can transact with confidence based on measurable data.

### Efficient Collection Management

For creators who own multiple pieces of analog gear, deciding which unit fits a particular production is often based on memory and intuition alone. Having each piece's acoustic profile documented as data makes the selection process dramatically more efficient.
